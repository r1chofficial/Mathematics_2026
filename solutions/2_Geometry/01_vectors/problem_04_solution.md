# Exercise 4. Dot Product and Perpendicularity

## Problem

For $u=(1,2,-1)$ and $v=(2,-1,3)$ compute $u\cdot v$ and determine whether the vectors are perpendicular. Then find $a$ so that $w=(2,-1,a)$ is perpendicular to $u$, and check the result.

## Solution

**Dot product.** Multiply matching coordinates and add:

$$
u\cdot v=1\cdot2+2\cdot(-1)+(-1)\cdot3=2-2-3=-3
$$

Two nonzero vectors are perpendicular exactly when their dot product is $0$. Since $u\cdot v=-3\neq0$, the vectors $u$ and $v$ are not perpendicular. The negative sign even shows that the angle between them is obtuse.

**Finding a.**

$$
u\cdot w=1\cdot2+2\cdot(-1)+(-1)\cdot a=-a=0\quad\Rightarrow\quad a=0
$$

So $w=(2,\ -1,\ 0)$.

**Check.** $u\cdot w=2-2+0=0$, so $u$ and $w$ are perpendicular.
